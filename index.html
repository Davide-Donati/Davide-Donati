01

Research

Variational methods
for complex materials.

My research lies in the Calculus of Variations, with particular attention to Γ-convergence, free-discontinuity problems, homogenisation, and models arising in fracture mechanics and structured deformations.

I am interested in variational problems involving singular perturbations, cohesive surface energies, generalised BV/BD spaces, and the passage from microscopic to effective macroscopic descriptions.

  • Free discontinuity
  • Homogenisation
  • BV / BD / GBD
  • Fracture
  • Phase transitions
  • Structured deformations
