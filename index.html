Davide Donati

Postdoctoral researcherTechnical University of Munich

I am currently a postdoc in the research group of Leonard Kreutz at TUM, Munich. Previously, I was a Phd student in Analysis at SISSA, Trieste, where I obtained my PhD under the supervision of Andrea Braides and Gianni Dal Maso. So far, my interest has been mainly devoted to the Calculus of Variations, with a particular attention to homogenisation problems, fine properties of functions of generalised bounded deformation, and models for phase transitions.
