Calculus of variations

Research

My research is in the calculus of variations, with a focus on Γ-convergence, free-discontinuity problems, and the analysis of variational models for materials.

  1. 01

    Free-discontinuity problems

    I study variational energies with bulk and surface contributions, including cohesive fracture and structured deformations. My work concerns relaxation, integral representation, and asymptotic behaviour in spaces of functions with discontinuities.

    • Free discontinuity
    • Cohesive fracture
    • Structured deformations
    Structured deformations for energies with general surface termsHomogenization of vectorial free-discontinuity functionals with cohesive type surface terms
  2. 02

    Functions of bounded variation and deformation

    I am interested in the fine properties of BV and BD functions and their generalised counterparts. Topics include derivative measures, function spaces adapted to cohesive energies, and Korn–Poincaré–Sobolev inequalities.

    • BV and BD
    • GBV and GBD
    • Fine properties
    A new space of generalised vector-valued functions of bounded variationA matrix-valued measure associated to the derivatives of a function of generalised bounded deformationA remark on the Korn–Poincaré–Sobolev inequality for functions of generalised bounded deformation
  3. 03

    Higher-order phase transitions

    I work on singular perturbation models involving higher-order derivatives. Through Γ-convergence, I study the relation between diffuse-interface energies and limiting interfacial models, including anisotropic materials.

    • Singular perturbations
    • Higher-order energies
    • Anisotropy
    Higher-order singular perturbation models for phase transitionsSingular perturbations models in phase transitions for anisotropic higher-order materials
  4. 04

    Homogenisation and nonlocal models

    I study effective variational models for heterogeneous media and the passage from nonlocal energies to local functionals. This includes elliptic homogenisation, free-discontinuity functionals, and convolution-type approximations.

    • Homogenisation
    • Nonlocal-to-local limits
    • Γ-convergence
    Another look at elliptic homogenizationΓ-convergence of convolution-type functionals for free discontinuity problemsΓ-convergence and homogenisation for free discontinuity functionals with linear growth in the space of functions with bounded deformation
