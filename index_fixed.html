Research

Variational methods
for complex materials.

I am a fourth (and last) year PhD student at SISSA, Trieste, where I have been working under the supervisions of Professors Andrea Braides and Gianni Dal Maso. I have been mainly working on variational problems involving spaces of discontinuous functions, with a particular attention to models arising in fracture mechanics and models for phase transitions.

I am quite thrilled about the study of functional properties of spaces of (G)BV/(G)BD functions and a large portion of my efforts as a PhD student has been devote to the study of their fine properties.

  • Free discontinuity
  • Homogenisation
  • BV / BD / GBD
  • Fracture
  • Phase transitions
  • Structured deformations
